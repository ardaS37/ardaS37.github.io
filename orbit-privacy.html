ORBIT Privacy Policy

Effective November 20, 2026

This policy applies to ORBIT, the Android puzzle game published by Sapsoft under the package name com.sapsoft.orbit.

Gameplay and saved data

ORBIT works without an account. The game stores your current run, scores, settings, virtual diamonds, themes, boosters, and daily wheel activity in its private storage on your device. Gameplay code uses those records to resume your run and keep your progress. Sapsoft does not receive these saved records on a game server.

Advertising

ORBIT uses Unity LevelPlay, including ironSource Ads and Unity Ads, to show interstitial and optional rewarded ads. When advertising is available, these services may collect and share device or app identifiers such as the Android advertising ID, IP address and approximate location, device and app information, ad views and interactions, and diagnostic or performance data. Unity’s SDK may also process other available advertising or measurement signals according to the provider policy linked below.

Unity and its advertising and measurement partners use this information to deliver and measure ads, verify rewards, maintain and improve the service, and detect fraud. Depending on applicable settings and choices, providers may use the data to personalize advertising. Unity states that its advertising SDKs encrypt collected data in transit.

  • Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy
  • ironSource Ads data disclosures
  • Unity Ads data disclosures

Retention and your choices

ORBIT keeps local progress while the game is installed and its storage is retained. You can remove local progress by clearing ORBIT's app storage in Android Settings. Use Android's advertising privacy settings to manage your advertising identifier and available advertising choices. Unity and its providers retain advertising information under their own policies. Unity’s privacy policy explains how to request access to or deletion of data held by Unity.
