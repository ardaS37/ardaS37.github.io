Privacy Policy
All That Matters

Last updated: 11 September 2026

About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how All That Matters, the mobile game provided by Arda Saplıoğlu, handles information. Contact: ardasaplioglu37@gmail.com.

Information handled by the game

All That Matters works offline and does not require an online account. The game does not collect or send your gameplay information to servers operated by the developer. It does not include advertising, tracking or analytics services.

Local game data

The game stores profile names, level progress, collected hearts, achievements, discovered secrets, bonus scores, story progress and sound and touch-control preferences locally on your device. These records are used to save your progress and apply your settings. A local profile is not an online account.

Sharing and device services

The developer does not sell or share your local game data. Google Play, Android and your device provider may process installation, diagnostic or device-backup information under their own policies and your device settings. Device backups may retain or restore local game data; the game does not operate its own cloud-save service.

Support messages

If you email the developer, your email address and the information you choose to send will be used to respond to your request. Messages are handled through the email service used by the developer. Please do not include sensitive information that is unnecessary for your request. Support correspondence is retained only as needed to resolve the request, maintain relevant support records or meet applicable legal obligations. You may request its deletion by email.

Retention and deletion

Local progress remains on your device until it is reset or removed. Reset progress in the game settings clears the active profile’s gameplay progress; it does not remove all profiles or device preferences. To remove all locally stored game data, clear the app’s storage in Android settings. Uninstalling normally removes local app data, but device backups may retain copies. Manage backup copies through your device or account settings.

Children’s privacy

The game does not request personal information from children or provide chat, advertising or online account registration. If you believe a child has sent personal information in a support message, contact the developer so that it can be reviewed and deleted as appropriate.

Security

Game saves are stored in the app’s local storage. Keep your device secure and use its available access controls. No storage or communication method can guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the game or its data practices change. The latest version will display an updated date. Material changes will be communicated where required.
